{% comment %} Payslip. One per employee, or several stacked with a page break between for the whole-run PDF. Laid out to satisfy BCEA section 33, which sets out what an employee is entitled to be given in writing: the employer's name and address, the employee's name and occupation, the period, the remuneration in money, every deduction, and the actual amount paid. The hours and rates are there too, since a wage worker cannot check the arithmetic without them. Table-based like every other PDF here -- xhtml2pdf supports a subset of CSS 2.1, no flexbox and no grid.
